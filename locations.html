Questions

Locations FAQs

Do you have offices outside Karachi?

Yes. Alongside our Karachi head office and workshop, we run regional offices in Lahore and Islamabad to support clients and installations in those regions.

Can you install a stall in a city where you don't have an office?

Yes. Stalls are fabricated at our Karachi workshop and transported for installation anywhere in Pakistan, including cities such as Multan, Faisalabad, Bahawalpur, Sialkot, Peshawar and Quetta.

Does the city affect exhibition stall cost?

Fabrication cost stays the same regardless of city, but transport distance from Karachi and local venue access can affect the total installed cost. See our exhibition stall cost guide for details.
