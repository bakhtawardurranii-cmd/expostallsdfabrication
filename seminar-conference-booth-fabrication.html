Booths For Seminars And Conferences

Seminars and conferences need structures that welcome guests, process registrations and give sponsors a clear place to meet delegates. Expo Stalls Fabrication designs, fabricates and installs these booths in-house, with our own crew on site, for events across Pakistan.

This is one of the event types covered by our event stall and booth fabrication service. If your event also has a trade-show floor, we can build both.

Registration Desks That Handle The Queue

The first ten minutes of an event are the busiest, and a poor desk creates queues. A good registration desk is sized for your guest numbers and set up for the work:

  • Separate counters or sections for pre-registered and walk-in guests
  • Space for laptops, printers or tablets and a lanyard and badge area
  • Clear signage that guests can read from the entrance
  • Storage hidden inside the counter so materials do not clutter the front
  • Branding that matches the event, using our exhibition branding service

Sponsor And Partner Booths

Sponsors expect visibility in return for their support. We build sponsor booths in matched sizes and finishes, so the sponsor area looks planned rather than patched together, and each sponsor gets space for a logo, a counter and a few displays. For events that run every year, a reusable system saves money: see our modular exhibition booths for how a set of booths can be rearranged each time.

Venue Rules And Setup

Seminars often run in hotels and halls with limited setup time. Share the venue, load-in times and any rules on fixings and floors. We dry-assemble the structures in our workshop first so installation is quick, and our crew handles dismantling afterwards. Our exhibition stall checklist lists the details to confirm, and our installation page explains how setup and dismantling work.

Speaker, Delegate And Partner Touchpoints

A seminar has more touchpoints than a registration desk. Each one is a small structure that can be built to match the event:

  • Speaker check-in desk with a private area for briefing and materials.
  • Delegate kit counter for badges, programmes and bags, with storage behind it.
  • Partner and exhibitor tables for organisations that need space but not a full booth.
  • Branded backdrops for speaker photographs that also look good on camera and in social posts.
  • Directional signage from the entrance to each session room, using our exhibition branding service.

Frequently Asked Questions

What booths do seminars and conferences usually need?

Registration desks, information points, sponsor and partner booths, and branded backdrops. We design the set around your guest numbers and layout.

Can you build matched sponsor booths?

Yes. We build sponsor booths in consistent sizes and finishes, with each sponsor’s logo and branding on a clear panel.

How do you handle tight setup windows in hotels?

We dry-assemble the structures in our workshop, plan loading and access, and install with our own crew so setup is quick.

Can the booths be reused next year?

Yes. Modular and aluminium-frame booths are designed to be reconfigured and reused.

How early should we book?

At least two to three weeks before the event once the design is approved, and earlier for larger events.
