About Expo Centre Lahore

Expo Centre Lahore is on Abdul Haque Road, Johar Town, Lahore. Wikipedia lists it as completed in 2010 and owned by Pakistan Expo Centres Limited, with about 4,000 square metres of exhibition space. Venue details below come from public sources and can change, so always check the organiser's exhibitor manual.

Best Exhibition Stand Design In Lahore: What Works

  • Start with one clear message and one product to feature.
  • Use a tall, lit fascia so visitors see you across the hall.
  • Keep the front open and place the counter to the side.
  • Add a small meeting area if sales conversations matter.
  • Choose materials that suit your budget: see our exhibition stall materials guide.

What Does A Stall Cost In Lahore?

Our 10×10 ft stalls start from PKR 94,499 for a basic build, and a typical custom 10×10 ft stall is around PKR 170,000. That is roughly PKR 945 to PKR 1,700 per square foot. These are guides, not fixed quotes. See the full breakdown in our exhibition stall cost in Pakistan guide.
