Exhibition Stalls For Sialkot Exporters

Sialkot is an export city, known for sports goods, surgical instruments and leather and garment products. A Sialkot stand has one job: let a buyer inspect the product with confidence. Expo Stalls Fabrication designs and builds those stands in our Lahore workshop and installs them with our own crew.

Built For Export Buyers

Visiting buyers often compare many suppliers in a day. A good Sialkot stand makes the first thirty seconds count.

  • Product first. Display your best-selling lines at eye level, with detail and specifications close by.
  • Clear, bilingual graphics so international visitors can read categories and company details at a glance.
  • Catalogue and sample shelves built into the structure, so brochures and price lists are easy to find.
  • A seated meeting corner for negotiating orders away from the walkway.
  • Easy access for staff. We plan counters and storage with our exhibition stall design team around how your team will actually work.

Display Solutions By Product

Sports Goods

Ball walls, racks and mannequins for apparel, so the range reads as a collection rather than a pile.

Surgical Instruments

Lockable glass cases with focused lighting so fine detail is visible and items stay protected.

Leather & Garments

Hanging rails and shaped shelving with warm, even light, so material and stitching stand out.

Mixed Ranges

Exhibiting More Than Once A Year

Many Sialkot companies attend several fairs a year, so one-off builds can become expensive. A reusable system may suit better.

Custom Build

  • Designed around one stand and one brand
  • Best for a flagship launch or a large island stand
  • Higher one-off investment

Modular System

  • Reconfigured for different booth sizes, as explained in our modular exhibition booths page
  • Lower cost per show over time
  • Faster to assemble at the venue

If you are unsure which path fits your calendar, read shell scheme vs custom exhibition stall before you choose.

Trade Fairs Connected To Sialkot

The Sialkot Chamber of Commerce and Industry lists a TDAP International Exhibition for 2026 to 2027 on its trade-fair page, and textile-sector fairs have also been listed for Sialkot. Dates and venues change, so confirm them with the organiser, then send us the exhibitor manual. We check height limits, flooring and electrical rules before the stand is built. Our exhibition stall checklist shows what to confirm and when.

Graphics And Compliance

International buyers read quickly, so graphics should be simple: company name, product categories and a short line on what you make. Any certifications you want to show can go on a clean panel, not scattered across the stand. Before design is final, check the organiser rules on height, flooring and electrical use, and share them with us. Our guide to common fabrication mistakes explains what goes wrong when these details are left late.

Sialkot FAQs

Where are Sialkot exhibition stalls built?

They are designed and fabricated at our Lahore workshop, then transported to your venue and installed by our team.

Can you build lockable display cases for instruments or small goods?

Yes. We can build display cases and counters with lockable storage as part of the stand, subject to the design and budget.

Is a modular stand worth it for a Sialkot exporter?

If you exhibit several times a year, usually yes, because the system is reconfigured instead of rebuilt. A single flagship show may suit a custom build better.

Do you have a Sialkot office?

No. We serve Sialkot from our Lahore workshop and offices, coordinated with our Karachi head office.

How early should I contact you?

At least two to three weeks before the show once your booth size is confirmed, and earlier for large stands.
