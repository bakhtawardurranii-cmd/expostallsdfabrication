Fragrance & BeautyKarachi

Aroma 247 Exhibition Booth — Karachi

Client
Aroma 247
Industry
Perfumes & Fragrance
City
Karachi
Venue
Karachi Expo Centre
Booth Size
~18 sqm (194 sq ft)
Booth Type
Custom luxury booth

Design Concept

Designed as a premium, retail-inspired fragrance environment combining wood-effect finishes, soft illumination, white architectural framing and curved forms. Integrated illuminated product niches create a premium display environment for the fragrance collection, while a large digital display adds a further communication point and the curved architectural frame creates a distinctive, recognisable silhouette.

Materials & Fabrication

  • Wood-effect wall finishes
  • Custom product display shelving
  • Integrated LED illumination
  • Custom reception counter
  • Printed and digital graphics
  • Curved architectural structures

Fabrication focused on the curved architectural framework, illuminated fragrance shelving, reception counter and branded display surfaces, with particular attention to lighting integration to achieve the intended premium retail appearance.

Challenge

Creating a booth capable of presenting fragrance products in a premium retail-like environment while retaining the functionality required of a professional exhibition stand.

Solution

Illuminated product shelves were integrated directly into the architectural walls, with a curved white frame forming a distinctive branded enclosure, a digital display adding dynamic communication, and a reception counter providing a dedicated visitor interaction point.

Final Result

A premium contemporary fragrance environment with strong product visibility — the combination of warm wood-effect surfaces, integrated lighting, curved white architecture and digital display creates an upscale brand experience on the show floor.

Fragrance & BeautyLahore

Ahmed Almaghribi Perfumes Exhibition Stall — Lahore

Client
Ahmed Almaghribi Perfumes
Industry
Perfumes & Fragrance
City
Lahore
Venue
Lahore Expo Centre
Booth Size
~18 sqm (194 sq ft)
Booth Type
Large custom stall

Design Concept

Designed around a premium luxury fragrance environment, combining strong brand visibility with an elegant retail-inspired product presentation. White architectural surfaces, gold accents, illuminated product displays and structured vertical elements give the stand a sophisticated identity, while a sweeping overhead fascia creates strong presence from the exhibition floor.

Materials & Fabrication

  • Custom exhibition wall structures
  • Printed and display graphics
  • Illuminated product shelving
  • Decorative architectural panels
  • LED lighting
  • Gold-finish decorative elements

The stall required custom fabrication of the architectural structure, branded fascia, product-display units, counters and illuminated display areas, with the fabrication approach focused on achieving a refined retail-style finish suitable for a premium fragrance brand.

Challenge

Creating a stand that could communicate the luxury and sophistication of a fragrance brand while providing substantial product-display space and maintaining a visually open exhibition presence.

Solution

A luxury-focused custom exhibition environment was built around illuminated shelving, gold detailing and branded architectural elements, with product-display niches integrated throughout so the space could function as both a brand experience and a product showcase.

Final Result

A high-end, luxury fragrance presentation with strong visibility across the exhibition floor — illuminated displays, gold architectural detailing, branded fascia and open visitor areas give the project a premium, retail-inspired appearance.

Manufacturing & IndustrialKarachi

TPL Thermoplast Exhibition Booth — Karachi

Client
TPL / Thermoplast (Pvt.) Ltd.
Industry
Manufacturing & Industrial
City
Karachi
Venue
Karachi Expo Centre
Booth Size
~36 sqm (388 sq ft)
Booth Type
Custom open stand

Design Concept

Developed around a bold corporate red-and-white identity designed for strong brand recognition across the exhibition floor. A large overhead branded fascia, rounded architectural corners, illuminated display panels and dedicated visitor-interaction areas give the booth a cohesive, highly recognisable presence.

Materials & Fabrication

  • Custom fabricated exhibition structures
  • Branded graphic panels
  • Illuminated display areas
  • Custom reception counters
  • Overhead fascia
  • Integrated LED lighting

The booth was custom fabricated around the company's corporate identity, including the curved architectural fascia, branded wall structures, display areas, counters and integrated lighting, then assembled on-site with branding, lighting and furniture.

Challenge

Creating a booth with high brand visibility while providing functional areas for product presentation, visitor interaction and corporate meetings.

Solution

A bold custom booth was developed around the red-and-white corporate identity, with a large overhead fascia for long-distance brand visibility and dedicated display areas and counters creating functional zones for visitors and product communication.

Final Result

A strong, highly recognisable corporate presence with clear sightlines, branded architectural elements and practical visitor areas throughout the finished booth.

Travel & TourismIslamabad

Over Asia Enterprise Exhibition Booth — Islamabad

Client
Over Asia Enterprise
Industry
Travel & Tourism
City
Islamabad
Venue
Pak-China Friendship Centre
Booth Size
~18 sqm (194 sq ft)
Booth Type
Custom open-side stand

Design Concept

Designed around a modern travel and tourism experience using blue, white and gold architectural elements. Destination-focused graphics, large digital displays and branded reception counters communicate the company's travel services, while curved and angled architectural elements give the booth a contemporary visual identity.

Materials & Fabrication

  • Custom exhibition wall structures
  • Printed branding graphics
  • Digital display screens
  • Custom reception counters
  • Decorative architectural panels
  • Visitor seating

The booth required custom fabrication of its architectural framework, branded walls, counters, display structures and graphic surfaces, developed specifically around the company's travel and tourism identity rather than a generic modular system.

Challenge

Communicating a wide range of travel destinations and services within a compact exhibition environment without creating visual clutter.

Solution

Large-format destination graphics, digital screens and clearly branded architectural surfaces communicated the company's offering, with open visitor areas and dedicated seating creating a comfortable environment for travel consultations.

Final Result

A welcoming, contemporary travel-brand environment with strong visual communication — blue-and-white branding, gold architectural detailing, destination imagery and digital displays give the stand a distinctive tourism-focused identity.
