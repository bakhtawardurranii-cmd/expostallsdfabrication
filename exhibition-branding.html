What Is Exhibition Branding?

Exhibition branding is the design and production of backdrops, graphics, signage, logos, product displays and wayfinding that carry your brand identity across the physical structure of your stall.

A well-built stall can still fall flat if the branding feels bolted on. We plan branding alongside the design and 3D stage — not as a final layer — so colours, signage placement, lighting and graphics are considered together with the structure itself.

What's Included

Backdrops

Feature walls and fascia graphics that anchor your stall's identity.

Graphics

Large-format printed and digital graphics across panels and displays.

Signage

Overhead, fascia and directional signage sized for visibility on the aisle.

Logos

Logo placement and lighting treated as a design decision, not an afterthought.

Product Displays

Shelving and cabinets branded to match the rest of the stand.

Wayfinding

Clear direction into meeting areas, product zones and service counters.

Printed Panels

Durable printed surfaces fitted to structural panels and backdrops.

Digital Displays

Screens integrated into the stall design where they support the brand story.

Brand Consistency Across The Stall

Consistency is what separates a branded exhibition stall from a structure with a logo printed somewhere on it. We check colour accuracy, typography, signage scale and logo placement against your brand guidelines at the same 3D design stage where the structure itself is approved — so nothing is left to guesswork once fabrication begins. Read more about how this fits into the wider process on our 3D exhibition booth design page.

Timeline

Branding is generally planned in parallel with stall design and fabrication rather than as a separate project stage, so it rarely adds to your overall timeline. Final artwork is best confirmed at least two weeks before your fabrication date to allow time for printing and quality checks.

Branding FAQs

What does exhibition branding include?

Exhibition branding covers backdrops, printed and digital graphics, signage, logos, product displays, wayfinding and lighting, applied consistently across the stall.

Can you apply branding to a stall you didn't fabricate?

Yes. If your stall structure already exists, we can design and fit signage, graphics and branding elements to it directly.

Do you design the branding or just produce it?

Both. We can design branding elements from your existing brand guidelines, or produce and fit artwork you already have ready.

What formats do you print for exhibition stalls?

We handle large-format printed panels and backdrops as well as digital displays, depending on what suits your stall design and budget.

How do you keep branding consistent across a large stall?

Branding is planned alongside the 3D design stage, so colours, signage placement and graphics are checked together before fabrication, rather than added on afterwards.
