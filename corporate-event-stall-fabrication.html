Stalls And Booths For Corporate Events

A corporate or company event puts your brand in front of customers, partners, investors or your own staff, so the stalls and booths have to look consistent and work smoothly. Expo Stalls Fabrication designs, fabricates and installs them in-house, with our own crew on site, across Pakistan.

This page covers events run by businesses. For broader event types, see our hub on event stall and booth fabrication, or for trade shows our exhibition stall fabrication service.

Keeping The Brand Exact

Corporate teams care about logos, colours and consistency. We work from your brand files, match colours on printed and painted surfaces, and show you the result in a 3D render before anything is built. Our exhibition branding service covers signage, graphics and backdrops, and our in-house printing and finishing keep every piece on brand. If your marketing team uses brand guidelines, send them with the brief and we will follow them.

Choosing The Right Stand For The Moment

Launch Stand

  • Built around one product or announcement
  • Backdrop, lighting and display designed together
  • Often custom, for a single dramatic reveal

Everyday Event Booth

  • Registration, information or sponsor use
  • Repeatable across events and cities
  • Well suited to recycled aluminium frames and modular builds

Not sure which suits you? Our guide to shell scheme vs custom stalls explains the trade-offs in cost, time and impact.

Hotels, Convention Spaces And Offices

Corporate events often happen in hotel ballrooms, convention spaces or office atriums. These venues usually set strict rules on access, loading, floors and fixings. Before the design is final, we ask about:

  • Loading route, lift sizes and permitted move-in times
  • Floor protection and fixing rules
  • Power points, ceiling height and lighting
  • The window for installation and dismantling
  • Whether the structure will be reused for another event

Our exhibition stall checklist and guide to fabrication mistakes show what to confirm early.

Approvals And Accountability

Corporate projects usually need internal sign-off. We provide a floor plan, a render and a quote against the final drawing so approvers can see what they are approving. During the build you deal with one team, which is the advantage of in-house fabrication, and our crew is on site for installation and dismantling.

Roadshows And Multi-City Events

Companies that take an event to several cities need stands that look the same in each venue. We build roadshow kits that pack flat, go up quickly and can be repaired on the road, and we can install in each city from the nearest workshop. We build in Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad and Multan and deliver from the nearest workshop. See our city pages for Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad and Multan, or the full list on our locations page. See also modular exhibition booths for how reusable systems keep the cost per city down.

Making The Event Look Designed

Small details decide whether a corporate event looks designed or assembled. We match colour across print, paint and laminate, keep logo placement consistent, hide cables and storage inside the structure, and place lighting so product and people look good in photographs and video. These choices begin in our exhibition stall design stage and are checked in the render before fabrication.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between a corporate event and a company event?

Corporate events are usually external, such as launches and conferences. Company events are often internal, such as annual days and town halls. We build stalls and booths for both.

Can you match our brand guidelines exactly?

Yes. Send your logo files, colours and guidelines, and we will follow them on printed and painted surfaces, then confirm with a 3D render before building.

Can you work to a tight installation window in a hotel or convention centre?

Yes. We plan access, loading and assembly around the window, and dry-assemble the structure in our workshop first so installation is quick.

Do you build reusable stands for roadshows?

Yes. Modular and aluminium-frame structures can be reconfigured and reused across cities.

How soon should we contact you?

At least two to three weeks before the event once the design is approved, and earlier for large builds.
