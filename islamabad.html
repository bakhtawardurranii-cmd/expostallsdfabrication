Exhibition Stalls In Islamabad

Expo Stalls Fabrication runs a regional office in Islamabad, at Enclave Mall, coordinating design approval, delivery and on-site installation for exhibitors in the capital. Fabrication takes place at our Karachi workshop before components are transported to Islamabad.

Islamabad's exhibition scene leans towards corporate events, government-adjacent expos, diplomatic and embassy-sector functions, and technology or trade showcases — a different mix from Karachi's industrial trade fairs or Lahore's retail-heavy calendar. Our regional office handles the local logistics that come with that kind of client base.

Why Exhibit In Islamabad

  • The capital's calendar of corporate, government-adjacent and diplomatic events.
  • A growing base for technology, travel and services exhibitors.
  • A regional office for direct local coordination and site visits.
  • Central access to clients across the twin cities of Islamabad and Rawalpindi.

Islamabad Venues

Pak-China Friendship Centre is one of Islamabad's main venues for trade fairs, exhibitions and public expos. We also deliver and install stalls at hotel convention spaces and corporate venues used for product launches, conferences and smaller trade events across the capital, coordinating hall access and move-in schedules directly with organisers.
