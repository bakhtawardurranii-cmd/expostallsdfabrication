What Is Exhibition Stall Installation?

Exhibition stall installation covers everything between a fabricated stall leaving the workshop and it being fully assembled, wired and show-ready at the venue — plus dismantling once the event ends.

It's easy to underestimate this stage until something goes wrong with venue access, a delivery slot, or a fascia that doesn't sit flush after transport. We treat installation as its own discipline — with its own coordination, checklist and crew — rather than an afterthought once fabrication is done.

What's Included

Transport

Careful packing and transport of fabricated components to the venue.

Venue Coordination

Working with organiser schedules, loading docks and access restrictions.

Move-In

Bringing components onto the show floor within the allocated build window.

On-Site Assembly

Structural assembly, panelling and fitting exactly as fabricated.

Electrical Coordination

Liaising with venue electricians for power, lighting and screen connections.

On-Site Supervision

A team member present through build and, where needed, through the show.

Dismantling

Careful breakdown of the stall once the exhibition closes.

Post-Show Removal

Clearing all material from the venue within the organiser's timeframe.

Installation Timelines

Timing depends on booth size and complexity. A small modular booth can typically be installed within a few hours; larger custom or double-deck stands usually need one to two full build days to allow for structural assembly, electrical work and finishing checks before the show opens. Dismantling is generally quicker than build, but still needs to fit within the organiser's move-out window.

Safety & Supervision

Every installation is supervised on-site, with attention to secure structural assembly, safe electrical connections and clear pathways for visitors and staff. Our crew follows venue-specific access and safety requirements set by the organiser, and remains reachable for any adjustments needed once the show is open.

Installation FAQs

What does exhibition stall installation include?

Installation covers transport to the venue, venue coordination, move-in, on-site assembly, electrical coordination, supervision during the show, and dismantling and removal once it ends.

Can you install a stall you didn't fabricate?

Yes. If your stall was fabricated elsewhere, our team can still handle transport, on-site installation, supervision and dismantling as a standalone service.

How long does installation take at the venue?

Move-in and installation time depends on booth size and complexity — a small modular booth can be installed in a few hours, while larger custom or double-deck stands typically need one to two full days.

Do you handle electrical coordination for lighting and screens?

Yes. Our team coordinates power and lighting connections with the venue's electrical contractor as part of installation.

What happens after the exhibition ends?

Once the show closes, our crew dismantles the stall and removes all material from the venue within the timeframe set by the organiser.

Do you install stalls outside Karachi?

Yes. Installation is carried out Pakistan-wide, including Lahore, Islamabad, Multan, Faisalabad, Bahawalpur, Sialkot, Peshawar and Quetta.
