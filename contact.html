Questions

Contact FAQs

What information should I include when requesting a quote?

It helps to share your booth size or venue allocation, event name and dates, city, and any reference images or brand guidelines you already have. We can still help if you only have a rough idea.

How quickly will I get a response?

Send your details through the form, WhatsApp or call directly during working hours, and our team will follow up to discuss your project.

Can I visit your workshop or office?

Yes. Our Karachi office and workshop, along with our Lahore and Islamabad offices, can be visited by appointment — call or WhatsApp ahead to arrange a time.

Do you take on projects outside Karachi, Lahore and Islamabad?

Yes. We install exhibition stalls across Pakistan, including Multan, Faisalabad, Bahawalpur, Sialkot, Peshawar and Quetta.
