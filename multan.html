Exhibition Stalls In Multan

Multan is a trade and agricultural centre in southern Punjab, and many of its exhibitors sell goods people want to see, handle or taste. Expo Stalls Fabrication designs and builds stands for them at our Multan workshop and installs them at the event with our own crew.

Built In Multan, Not Shipped In

With a workshop in Multan, there is no long haul between the build and your venue. Carpentry, metalwork, printing and finishing take place locally, so changes can be made in the workshop within hours, and the people who built your stand install it. The same workshop supports exhibitors in the surrounding region, including Bahawalpur. For how the build works, see how expo stall fabrication works, and for why who builds the stand matters, read in-house vs outsourced stall fabrication.

Seasonal Events Shape The Stand

Multan hosts an annual mango festival each summer, alongside business and regional events through the year. A seasonal event is different from an indoor trade show: stands are busier, warmer and used by many more visitors per hour. We plan for that with durable surfaces, clear walkways and counters that are easy to wipe down. For help choosing finishes, see our exhibition stall materials guide.

Produce Stand Or Craft Stand?

The right layout depends on what you are showing. Produce wants volume and freshness. Crafts want quiet and good light.

Produce & Agriculture

  • Counters at working height for displays, sampling and packing
  • Wipe-clean surfaces and easy storage behind the counter
  • Bold signage so visitors identify the product from a distance

Crafts & Ceramics

  • Shelving and plinths that give each piece its own space
  • Focused lighting that shows colour and glaze
  • Calmer layouts that invite visitors to browse

Our exhibition stall design team prepares the floor plan around these needs before any build starts.

Choosing Materials For Heat And Handling

  • Laminated and sealed surfaces resist marks better than bare painted board, so they suit counters and shelving.
  • Light, reusable frames make sense if you attend festivals every year. See our recycled aluminium exhibition frames for reusable structure options.
  • Ventilated layouts keep the stand comfortable for staff in warm weather.
  • Tell us if the event is outdoors so we can specify fixings and materials accordingly.

Designing For A Busy Stand

Festival and trade stands in Multan can get crowded quickly. A few layout decisions keep visitors moving and staff in control:

  • Place the main counter to one side so a queue does not block the entrance.
  • Keep a clear route in and out, with product displays set back from the aisle.
  • Give staff a protected back area for storage, packing and breaks.
  • Set signage high enough to be seen over a crowd.

If you want a screen or see-through display, our transparent LED mesh booths show moving content without blocking the view, and our exhibition branding service covers signage and graphics.
