What Does A Stall Maker Do?

A stall maker takes a brief and turns it into a physical booth. The work covers four jobs:

  • Design: a floor plan and a 3D view of the stall, so you approve the look before anything is cut. See our 3D booth design.
  • Fabrication: building walls, counters, shelves and frames in a workshop. See exhibition stall fabrication.
  • Branding: printed graphics, lit logos and signage. See exhibition branding.
  • Installation: transport, setup at the venue and dismantling after the show. See exhibition stall installation.

Some companies do only one of these jobs and pass the rest to others. We do all four ourselves. Read why that matters in in-house vs outsourced fabrication.

Types Of Stalls We Make

  • Custom stalls built from wood, MDF, acrylic and aluminium to your design. See the custom stall builder page.
  • modular exhibition booths that can be reconfigured and reused at later shows.
  • transparent LED mesh booths for a see-through, lit look.
  • Lightweight reusable frames: recycled aluminium frames.
  • Kiosks and photo booths: kiosk and photo booth fabrication.
  • Event stalls: event stalls and booths for corporate events, seminars and convocations.

What Does A Stall Maker Charge?

Our 10×10 ft stalls start from PKR 94,499 for a basic build, and a typical custom 10×10 ft stall is around PKR 170,000. That is roughly PKR 945 to PKR 1,700 per square foot. These are guides, not fixed quotes. See the full breakdown in our exhibition stall cost in Pakistan guide.

Cost depends on size, materials, branding, lighting and timeline. Our 10×10 ft stall cost and cost per square foot guides explain each factor.

How We Work: From Brief To Installed Stall

  1. Send your show name, dates, booth size and logo.
  2. We send a layout and a 3D view.
  3. You approve the design and quote.
  4. We build in our workshop (usually 2 to 3 weeks from approval).
  5. We install, supervise and dismantle.
