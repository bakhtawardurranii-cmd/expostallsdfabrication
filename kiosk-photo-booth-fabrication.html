Kiosk Maker: What We Build

A kiosk is a compact, standalone display unit. We design, build and install custom kiosks and photo booths for brands, retail, events and exhibitions across Pakistan, all by one in-house team.

One team handles the drawing, the workshop build and the installation. Our team installed and dismantled over 200 stalls in 2026, and the same workshops build your kiosk. We build kiosks for:

  • Product displays and sampling counters.
  • Brand activations and launches.
  • Information, registration and sponsor desks at events. See event stall and booth fabrication.

More detail: kiosk maker in Pakistan and photo booth fabrication in Pakistan.

Photo Booth Fabrication

A photo booth gives your brand a place where visitors want to be photographed. We build custom photo booth structures, backdrops and branded frames to your design. Each one carries your logo, colours and lighting, and is built to be moved between venues.

Materials We Build Kiosks And Photo Booths With

We work in wood, MDF, acrylic and aluminium. Wood and MDF give a smooth painted or laminated body. Acrylic suits logos and lit panels. Aluminium keeps a unit light and easy to reuse. Not sure what to pick? Read our exhibition stall materials guide.

Kiosk And Photo Booth FAQs

What is the difference between a kiosk and a stall?

A kiosk is a small standalone unit that visitors approach from several sides. A stall or booth is a larger space with walls and floor area. We build both.

How long does a custom kiosk take?

Most custom stalls take two to three weeks from design approval to installation. Send your brief for a kiosk-specific timeline.

Can you build a photo booth to my brand design?

Yes. Send your logo and colours and we will design it, then build and install it.
