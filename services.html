How These Services Work Together

Most clients move through these services in order — but each one can also stand alone if you've already completed part of the work with someone else.

01

Design The Layout

We start with exhibition stall design — floor plan, visitor flow and branding direction.

02

Visualise It In 3D

The layout becomes a 3D render so you can approve the look before anything is built.

03

Fabricate In-House

Once approved, the design goes into fabrication at our Karachi workshop — custom or modular.

04

Branding — signage, graphics and displays — is fitted to carry your identity.

05

Install & Dismantle

Our team handles installation, on-site supervision and dismantling once the show ends.

Services FAQs

Do I need to book design and fabrication separately?

No. Design, fabrication and installation can be booked as one project with a single point of contact, or individually if part of the work is already done elsewhere.

Can I get just installation without design or fabrication?

Yes. If your stall is already designed or built, our team can handle transport, on-site installation, supervision and dismantling on their own.

Which service do I need if I've never exhibited before?

Most first-time exhibitors start with exhibition stall design, which sets the floor plan and look before anything is fabricated. From there we can fabricate and install the same design.

Do you provide these services outside Karachi?

Yes. Design and fabrication are based in Karachi, with regional offices in Lahore and Islamabad, and installation carried out across Multan, Faisalabad, Bahawalpur, Sialkot, Peshawar, Quetta and other cities.
