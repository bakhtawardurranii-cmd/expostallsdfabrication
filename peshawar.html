Exhibition Stalls In Peshawar

Peshawar is known for its gem and mineral trade, alongside marble, crafts and regional business. Expo Stalls Fabrication designs and fabricates stands for these exhibitors at our Lahore workshop, with coordination support from our Islamabad office, and installs them with our own crew.

Gems, Jewellery And Minerals

Peshawar is home to Namak Mandi, a large market for semi-precious stones, and gem, jewellery and mineral exhibitions have been held in the city, including events organised by the All Pakistan Commercial Exporters Association. Exhibitors in this trade carry small items of high value, so the stand has to show detail while keeping stock safe. That makes lighting, glass and lockable storage the core of the design rather than an extra.

Display Design For High-Value Items

  • Lockable glass cases at counter height, with removable trays for easy showing.
  • Focused lighting that brings out colour and clarity, with no glare on the glass.
  • Shallow shelves and velvet-lined surfaces so items sit on a backdrop that flatters them.
  • A staffed viewing counter where buyers can examine stones with a loupe.
  • Discreet storage built into the structure. Our 3D exhibition booth design service lets you check sight lines and counter positions before the build.

The venue and the organiser are responsible for security, so confirm their arrangements and tell us your requirements early. The materials guide explains finishes that suit glass and display work.

Lighting Makes Or Breaks A Gem Display

Stones and jewellery rely on light. Soft, even lighting hides sparkle and clarity; sharp, uncontrolled spotlights create glare. We plan lighting as part of the structure: warm accents inside cases, neutral light at the viewing counter and a clean ambient level so the stand looks consistent. Because glass reflects, we also plan where lights sit relative to the visitor, so reflections do not hide the items. This is covered at the design stage through our exhibition stall design service.

Peshawar FAQs

Where are Peshawar stalls built?

At our Lahore workshop, then transported to Peshawar and installed by our team, with our Islamabad office helping coordinate.

Can you build lockable glass display cases?

Yes. We can build glass-fronted cases and counters with lockable storage as part of the stand.

Do you provide security at the exhibition?

Event security is arranged by the organiser and venue. We design lockable display and storage into the stand.

Do you have a Peshawar office?

No. Our nearest office is in Islamabad, and our workshops are in Lahore and Karachi.

How soon should I contact you?

At least two to three weeks before the show, and earlier if the stand is large or has custom glass work.
