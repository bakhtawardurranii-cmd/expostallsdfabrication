Questions

Exhibition Stall Design & Fabrication — Answered Directly.

Real questions from clients planning their first — or fiftieth — exhibition stall.

What is exhibition stall fabrication?

Exhibition stall fabrication is the process of turning an approved booth design into a physical structure — carpentry, metalwork, graphics, lighting and display fittings — followed by delivery and installation at the exhibition venue.

How much does an exhibition stall cost in Pakistan?

A small 10×10 ft booth typically starts from around PKR 94,499, while larger custom and double-deck stalls can run into the hundreds of thousands depending on size, materials, branding and city. See our exhibition stall cost guide for a full breakdown.

How long does design and fabrication take?

Most custom stalls take between two and five weeks from design approval to installation, depending on size and complexity. Modular stalls can usually be turned around faster.

What's the difference between a modular and a custom exhibition stall?

A modular exhibition booth uses a pre-engineered, reusable frame system that is quicker and more affordable — ideal for brands that exhibit repeatedly. A custom stall is designed and built from scratch specifically for one event or brand, with branding and finishing tailored to it either way.

Do you handle installation and dismantling as well as fabrication?

Yes. Our scope includes transport, on-site installation, supervision during the exhibition and dismantling once the show ends, across Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad and other cities in Pakistan.

Can you fabricate a stall design created by another company?

Yes. We can fabricate and install a stall from an approved design or drawing supplied by your designer, provided the technical details are complete.
