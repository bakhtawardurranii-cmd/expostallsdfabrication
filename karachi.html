Exhibition Stalls In Karachi

Expo Stalls Fabrication is headquartered in Karachi, where our design team and fabrication workshop are based. If your exhibition is in Karachi, your stall is designed, built and installed by the same local team, with no transport lead time between workshop and venue.

Karachi is Pakistan's largest commercial city and the base for most of our documented projects, including our Aroma 247 and TPL Thermoplast case studies. Being based here means shorter lead times, easier site visits during fabrication, and direct coordination with venue teams on move-in day.

Why Exhibit In Karachi

  • Pakistan's largest business hub, with year-round trade fairs and consumer exhibitions.
  • Home to major national and international exhibitions across multiple industries.
  • The most direct access to our design team and fabrication workshop.
  • A strong base for brands looking to launch or scale into other Pakistani cities.

Expo Centre Karachi & Other Venues

Expo Centre Karachi, on University Road, is the city's primary large-scale exhibition venue, hosting major trade fairs and consumer exhibitions across industries throughout the year. We coordinate directly with venue teams on hall access, move-in and move-out schedules, and electrical requirements — the same process we follow at other Karachi venues and hotel exhibition spaces used for smaller trade events.

Local Installation & Transport

Because fabrication happens at our Karachi workshop, there's no long-distance transport involved for local exhibitions — components move directly from workshop to venue, which keeps installation timelines tight and gives us flexibility to handle last-minute adjustments during the build. Our crew handles move-in, on-site assembly, electrical coordination, supervision and dismantling for every Karachi project. See our installation page for full details.

Karachi FAQs

Which company provides exhibition stall fabrication in Karachi?

Expo Stalls Fabrication is headquartered in Karachi, where design, carpentry, metalwork, printing and finishing all take place in our own workshop before installation at the venue.

Do you fabricate exhibition stalls in Karachi?

Yes. Karachi is our head office and the location of our fabrication workshop, so every stall — whether it's being installed in Karachi or another city — is built here first.

Can you deliver and install a stall at Expo Centre Karachi?

Yes. We regularly deliver and install stalls at Expo Centre Karachi and other venues across the city, coordinating move-in schedules and venue access directly with organisers.

How much does an exhibition stall cost in Karachi?

Since fabrication happens locally, there's no added transport cost for Karachi projects. A 10×10 ft booth typically starts from around PKR 94,499 — see our cost guide for a full breakdown.

What booth sizes have you built for Karachi exhibitions?

We've fabricated everything from compact kiosk booths to large island stands for Karachi exhibitors, including the Aroma 247 and TPL Thermoplast projects featured in our portfolio.
