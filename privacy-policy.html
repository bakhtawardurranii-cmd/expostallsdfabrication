Privacy Policy

How We Handle Your Information.

Last updated: September 2026. This policy explains what information Expo Stalls Fabrication collects through this website and how it is used.

Who We Are

Expo Stalls Fabrication is an exhibition stall design, fabrication and installation company headquartered at Office 806, 8th Floor, Muhammadi Trade Tower, New Chali, Karachi, Pakistan. This policy covers the website at expostallsdfabrication.com and the "Get A Free Quote" enquiry form on it. Any questions about this policy can be sent to hello@expostallsdfabrication.com.

Information We Collect

When you submit our enquiry form, we collect the details you enter: your full name, phone/WhatsApp number, email address, city/venue and any project details you type into the message field. If you email or call us directly, we hold whatever information you choose to share in that message or call.

Like most websites, this site also collects limited technical and usage information automatically — for example pages viewed, general location (city/country level) and device/browser type — through the analytics tool described below.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your enquiry and prepare a quote for exhibition stall design, fabrication or installation.
  • To contact you by phone, WhatsApp or email about your project.
  • To understand how visitors use this website, so we can improve it.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use enquiry-form details for unrelated marketing without your consent.

Third-Party Services We Use

This site relies on a small number of third-party services to operate:

  • FormSubmit — processes and forwards enquiry-form submissions to our business email address. Form data passes through FormSubmit's servers to reach us.
  • Microsoft Clarity — collects anonymised usage analytics (page views, clicks, scroll behaviour) to help us understand how the site is used.
  • Google Fonts — loads the typefaces used on this site from Google's servers.

Each of these providers has its own privacy policy governing how it handles data on its platform.

How Long We Keep Information

We keep enquiry details for as long as needed to respond to your project and for a reasonable period afterwards for our own records, then delete or archive them. You can ask us to delete your information at any time by emailing hello@expostallsdfabrication.com.

Your Choices

You can choose not to submit the enquiry form and instead contact us by phone or WhatsApp at 0335 2198250. You may also ask us at any time what information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it.

Changes To This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time as our website or tools change. The "last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision.

Contact Us

For any privacy-related question, email hello@expostallsdfabrication.com or call/WhatsApp 0335 2198250.
