Exhibition Stalls For Faisalabad Textile Brands

Faisalabad is best known as a textile and manufacturing city, and its exhibitors need stands that show fabric, finish and range clearly. Expo Stalls Fabrication designs and fabricates these stands at our Lahore workshop and installs them at your venue with our own crew.

We do not have a permanent office in Faisalabad. Your stand is designed, built and quality-checked in one of our two workshops, usually the Lahore one for Faisalabad projects, so the people who build it are the people who install it. Our Lahore office and workshop in Johar Town is the base for most Faisalabad work.

Where Faisalabad Exhibitors Actually Show

Faisalabad companies exhibit in two places, and the stand has to work for both. For example, Faisalabad Expo 2026, organised by the Faisalabad Chamber of Commerce and Industry, was held at Expo Centre Lahore in July 2026.

  • A lighter, faster-to-install stand is usually the better fit

In Lahore

  • Large halls such as Expo Centre Lahore, where competing stands are many and close together
  • Higher ceilings and longer sight lines, so height and signage matter
  • Our Expo Centre Lahore stall design and cost guide explains what to plan for

Designing A Textile Stand That Sells

A textile buyer wants to touch the product, compare colours and sit down to talk. The stand should make that easy, not just look good from the aisle.

  • Swatch walls and hanging rails built into the structure so samples are visible and can be handled safely.
  • True-colour lighting over fabric displays. Warm or uneven light changes how shades read, which can cost you at the buying table.
  • A buyer meeting zone with seating and a small table, kept separate from the busiest part of the aisle.
  • Sample storage hidden inside counters and cupboards so catalogues and bags do not clutter the floor.
  • Clear category signage, for example garments, home textiles and fabric, so visitors find their range quickly.

Fabric-heavy stands also carry a lot of print. Our exhibition branding service covers backdrops, graphics and signage, and our exhibition stall materials guide helps you choose finishes that handle repeated handling.

Faisalabad FAQs

Where will my Faisalabad stall be built?

Stalls for Faisalabad exhibitors are normally built at our Lahore workshop, which is located in the basement of our Lahore office, and then transported and installed by our team.

Can you install at Expo Centre Lahore as well as in Faisalabad?

Yes. Many Faisalabad companies exhibit in both cities, and the same team handles installation at either venue.

What should a textile exhibition stand include?

Swatch walls or rails, true-colour lighting, a small meeting area, hidden sample storage and clear category signage. We design around how your buyers handle product.

How far ahead should a Faisalabad exhibitor book?

At least two to three weeks before the show, and earlier for large stands or busy exhibition months.

Do you have an office in Faisalabad?

No. We deliver and install there from our Lahore workshop and offices, and coordinate through our Karachi head office and Islamabad office.
