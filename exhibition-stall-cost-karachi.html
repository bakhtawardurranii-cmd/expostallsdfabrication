What Affects Cost In Karachi

  • Booth size and open sides.
  • Materials and finish.
  • Branding, lighting and furniture.
  • Transport and crew travel from the workshop to the venue. Our Karachi head office and workshop build for shows at Karachi Expo Centre, so transport to the venue is short. See our Expo Centre Karachi guide.
  • Timeline: urgent builds cost more.

What A Karachi Stall Quote Should Include

  • Design and 3D view.
  • Structure, carpentry and metalwork.
  • Standard finishes and graphics printing.
  • Basic lighting fixtures.
  • Transport, installation and dismantling.

Items often quoted separately include premium laminates, furniture, screens and venue-charged power. See exhibition stall cost in Pakistan.

Timeline And Urgency

Most builds take 2 to 3 weeks from design approval to installation. Rush jobs cost more, so approve your design early. See how expo stall fabrication works.
