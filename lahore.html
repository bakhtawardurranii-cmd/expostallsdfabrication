Exhibition Stalls In Lahore

Expo Stalls Fabrication runs a regional office in Lahore, based in Johar Town, coordinating design approval, delivery and on-site installation for Lahore exhibitors. Fabrication itself takes place at our Karachi workshop before components are transported to Lahore.

Lahore is one of Pakistan's most active exhibition cities, with a steady calendar of trade fairs, product launches and corporate events. Having a local office here means Lahore-based clients get in-person coordination without needing to deal directly with a workshop three provinces away.

Why Exhibit In Lahore

  • One of Pakistan's busiest exhibition and trade fair calendars.
  • A strong base for textile, retail, food and consumer brands.
  • A regional office in Johar Town for direct local coordination.
  • Central access to Punjab's wider business and retail market.

Expo Centre Lahore & Other Venues

Expo Centre Lahore, located in Johar Town — the same area as our regional office — is one of the city's main exhibition venues, hosting trade fairs and consumer exhibitions throughout the year. Being based nearby makes move-in coordination, venue access and last-minute on-site adjustments straightforward. We also deliver and install stalls at hotel exhibition spaces and other venues used for smaller Lahore trade events.

Local Installation & Transport

Stalls fabricated in Karachi are transported to Lahore ahead of the agreed build window, with our regional office managing local coordination — venue access, move-in scheduling and on-site supervision. Our crew handles assembly, electrical coordination, supervision through the show, and dismantling once it ends. See our installation page for the full process.
