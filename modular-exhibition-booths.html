What Are Modular Exhibition Booths?

Modular exhibition booths are built on a pre-engineered, reusable frame system that can be reconfigured into different sizes and layouts, rather than being designed and fabricated from scratch for every event.

Think of it as a kit of parts — panels, framework, lighting and graphics — engineered once and then rebuilt, resized or refreshed for each new exhibition. For brands that exhibit repeatedly, this removes the cost and lead time of starting from zero every time.

Benefits Of A Modular System

  • Reusable across events — the same core frame is reconfigured rather than rebuilt.
  • Faster fabrication — the frame system is already engineered and tested.
  • Reconfigurable layouts — panels and components can be rearranged for different booth sizes.
  • Lower cost per use — the investment is spread across multiple exhibitions.
  • Simpler transport and storage — modular components typically pack down more predictably than bespoke structures.

Modular vs Custom Exhibition Stall

Modular Booth FAQs

What is a modular exhibition booth?

A modular exhibition booth is built on a pre-engineered, reusable frame system that can be reconfigured into different sizes and layouts, rather than being designed and fabricated from scratch for every event.

Is a modular booth cheaper than a custom stall?

The first build is often similar in cost to a simple custom stall, but a modular booth becomes more cost-effective over repeat exhibitions since the frame is reused rather than rebuilt each time.

Can a modular booth be resized for different events?

Yes. Modular systems are designed to be reconfigured into different footprints and layouts, within the limits of the components fabricated for your kit.

How long does modular booth fabrication take?

Modular booths are generally quicker to fabricate than fully custom stalls, since the frame system is already engineered and tested.

Do you store modular booths between exhibitions?

This can be arranged as part of your project — ask our team about storage and transport options between events when you request a quote.
