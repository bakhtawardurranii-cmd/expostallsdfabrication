What Makes A Stall Custom?

A custom stall starts from your brief, not a ready-made frame. Walls, counters, shelves, lighting and graphics are drawn for your products and your booth size, then built to fit.

Compare it with a ready-made option in shell scheme vs custom stall.

Materials We Build Custom Stalls From

  • Wood and plywood for strong counters and shelves.
  • MDF for smooth painted or laminated fascias and curved panels.
  • Acrylic for logos, shelves and backlit panels.
  • Aluminium for light frames that can be reused.

Details in our exhibition stall materials guide.

How A Custom Stall Is Built

  1. Brief: booth size, open sides, products, brand files.
  2. Design: layout and 3D view, revised until you approve.
  3. Fabrication: built in sections in our workshop.
  4. Dry fit: assembled and checked before shipping.
  5. Installation: set up, tested and supervised at the venue.

Custom Stall Cost In Pakistan

Our 10×10 ft stalls start from PKR 94,499 for a basic build, and a typical custom 10×10 ft stall is around PKR 170,000. That is roughly PKR 945 to PKR 1,700 per square foot. These are guides, not fixed quotes. See the full breakdown in our exhibition stall cost in Pakistan guide.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a custom stall?

A stall designed and built around your brand, products and booth size, instead of a standard ready-made structure.

How much does a custom stall cost in Pakistan?

A typical custom 10×10 ft stall is around PKR 170,000, and our basic 10×10 ft builds start from PKR 94,499. Final price depends on materials, branding and timeline.

Can you build from my own design?

Yes. We can build from your design, and we review it for technical and venue requirements before fabrication.

How long does a custom build take?

Usually 2 to 3 weeks from approved design to installation.

Do custom stalls last for more than one show?

They can, depending on the design. Modular and aluminium-frame stalls are built for reuse.
