Exhibition Stalls In Quetta

Quetta is the commercial centre of Balochistan, with exhibitors in fruit and dry fruit, minerals, marble and crafts. Expo Stalls Fabrication designs and fabricates stands for Quetta at our Karachi workshop, which is the closer of our two workshops, and installs them with our own crew.

Planning For A Long Delivery

A long road changes how a stand should be built. Anything that arrives damaged or missing a part is hard to replace on site, so we plan for the journey.

Venues And Space

Quetta does not yet have a dedicated expo centre. A proposed one was put on hold in March 2026, so exhibitions may be held in hotels, halls and other event spaces. Rooms of different sizes and ceiling heights mean a stand should be planned to fit the actual space, not a generic hall. Share photos and measurements early, and we will adjust the design. Custom Or Modular For Quetta?

Custom Build

  • Designed for one venue and brand
  • Best when the layout needs specific shapes or joinery
  • Heavier and slower to ship

Modular Or Aluminium

  • Flat-packs more easily, which helps on a long trip. See modular exhibition booths
  • Lighter to move and easier to repair or reconfigure
  • A reusable choice, such as recycled aluminium frames

For a side-by-side view of the choices, read shell scheme vs custom exhibition stall.

Support During The Event

Because we install our own stands, our team is on site during move-in and can check the stand before the event opens. Tell us beforehand about power points, lighting and any screens so we can design cable routes into the structure instead of adding them late. After the event, we dismantle and pack the stand, and can advise on whether components can be reused. For a view of the stages before and after the build, see our exhibition stall checklist and guide to comparing fabricators.

Pricing For Quetta Exhibitors

A 10×10 ft booth typically starts from around PKR 94,499, and every project is quoted against the final drawing. Longer transport to Quetta is reflected in the quote, so share your dates and venue early. Because of the distance, aim for the longer end of our booking window. Quetta FAQs

Where are Quetta exhibition stalls built?

At our Karachi workshop, which is in the basement of our Karachi head office. They are then transported to Quetta and installed by our team.

Does Quetta have an expo centre?

Not yet. A proposed expo centre was put on hold in March 2026, so exhibitions may take place in hotels, halls and other venues. Check the venue with your organiser.

Is a modular stand better for Quetta?

Often, because it packs flatter and is lighter to transport. A custom stand is still possible when the design needs it.

Do you have an office in Quetta?

No. We serve Quetta from our Karachi head office and workshop.

How early should I book for Quetta?

At least two to three weeks before the show, and ideally earlier because of the distance.
