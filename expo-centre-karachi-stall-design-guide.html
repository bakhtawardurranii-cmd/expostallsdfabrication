About Expo Centre Karachi

Karachi Expo Centre is on Main University Road, Gulshan-e-Iqbal, Karachi. According to Wikipedia it has six halls: Halls 1 to 3 are 2,230 square metres each, and Halls 4 to 6 are 3,345 square metres each. Venue details below come from public sources and can change, so always check the organiser's exhibitor manual.

Planning A Stall For Karachi Expo Centre

  1. Book your space and download the exhibitor manual from the organiser.
  2. Note your booth size, open sides, height limits and power allowance.
  3. Send the manual, your logo and products to your fabricator.
  4. Approve a 3D design at least 2 to 3 weeks before move-in.
  5. Confirm move-in, move-out and passes for your crew.

Use our exhibition stall checklist and avoid common stall fabrication mistakes.

Stall Design Tips For Large Halls

  • In big halls, a tall logo or lit fascia helps visitors find you from the aisle.
  • Keep the front open so people can walk in.
  • Plan lighting and power with the organiser early.
  • Use branded floor, back wall and counter so the stall reads clearly from a distance.

See exhibition stall design and exhibition branding.

Mistakes To Avoid At Large Venues

  • Starting design before reading the exhibitor manual.
  • Branding that is too small to read from the aisle.
  • Late artwork, which delays printing.
  • Not agreeing move-in times and passes for the crew.
