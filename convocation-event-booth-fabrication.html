Booths And Stalls For Convocations

A convocation is a high-volume event with a fixed moment: graduates and their families arrive together, register quickly and want photographs. Expo Stalls Fabrication builds the counters, photo walls and booths that support this, in-house, and installs them with our own crew at universities, colleges and halls across Pakistan.

It is one of the event types in our event stall and booth fabrication service, alongside corporate events and seminars.

What A Convocation Needs

Registration Counters

Several desks so graduates and guests do not share one queue.

Regalia And Gown Counters

Counters with storage behind them for distribution and return.

Photo Walls

Branded backdrops that give families a place for photographs.

Alumni And Sponsor Booths

Matched booths for alumni offices, partners and sponsors.

Information Points

Help desks with clear signage for seating, schedules and parking.

Souvenir Stalls

Counters for keepsakes, publications and merchandise.

Designing For A Crowd

Convocations are quiet for hours and then everyone arrives at once. The layout should absorb that surge:

  • Separate lines for graduates, guests and staff, marked with clear signs
  • Counters at the right height and length for the expected queue
  • Space behind the counter for storage and for staff to move
  • A route from entrance to registration to seating that does not cross the photo area
  • A written plan shared with the organiser, supported by a 3D render so everyone can see the layout

Photo Walls Families Remember

The photo wall is often the most-used structure at a convocation. A good one is wide enough for groups, well lit and finished so colours look right in pictures. We print and finish backdrops in-house, and build frames that stay stable in a crowd. For help choosing materials, see our exhibition stall materials guide.

Getting The Institution’s Identity Right

Universities care about their crest, colours and typefaces. Send your brand files and we will follow them on printed and painted surfaces, then check the result in a render before building. Our exhibition branding service covers backdrops, signage and wayfinding, so the whole event looks consistent.

Campus Halls, Lawns And Auditoriums

Convocations take place in auditoriums, halls and sometimes on campus lawns. Tell us the space, the surface and the weather exposure, and we will specify structure and fixings to suit. If the same ceremony repeats every year, consider a reusable system, such as modular exhibition booths or recycled aluminium frames, so next year’s event costs less to set up. Our installation team handles setup and dismantling around the ceremony schedule.

Working With A University Team

Convocations usually involve several departments: the registrar, student affairs, procurement and marketing. We provide a floor plan, a render and a quote against the final drawing, so each department can see what it is approving. We follow brand rules supplied by your marketing team and can itemise the quote if your procurement process needs it. We serve universities and institutes in Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, Multan and other cities. See our Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad and Multan pages, or our locations page.

Frequently Asked Questions

What do you build for a convocation?

Registration counters, regalia counters, photo walls, alumni and sponsor booths, information points and souvenir stalls, depending on your brief.

Can you match our university’s branding?

Yes. Send your crest, colours and brand guidelines and we will follow them, then confirm with a 3D render before building.

Can you set up on a campus lawn?

Yes, once we know the surface and weather exposure. We specify structure and fixings to suit.

Can the structures be reused next year?

Yes. Modular and aluminium-frame structures can be reconfigured and reused for later ceremonies.

How soon should we contact you?

At least two to three weeks before the ceremony once the design is approved, and earlier for large events.
