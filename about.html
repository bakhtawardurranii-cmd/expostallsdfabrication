Who We Are

Expo Stalls Fabrication is an exhibition stall design and fabrication company headquartered in Karachi, Pakistan, with regional offices in Lahore and Islamabad. We design, fabricate and install custom and modular exhibition stalls for brands exhibiting across the country.

The name isn't a marketing choice — it describes what we actually do. Design happens in-house, fabrication happens in our own Karachi workshop, and installation is handled by our own crews at venues nationwide. Nothing is passed between separate vendors along the way, which is the main reason clients come back to us for repeat exhibitions.

Our Story

Expo Stalls Fabrication grew out of hands-on work on the exhibition floor, long before it became a formally registered company.

2012

Began designing and fabricating exhibition stalls for clients in Karachi, working directly on the show floor and in the workshop.

2017

Formally registered the business with the Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan (SECP), reg. no. 0250176423979.

Since 2017

Opened regional offices in Lahore and Islamabad to support clients and installations across northern and central Pakistan.

Today

Design, fabricate and install exhibition stalls for clients across 9 cities in Pakistan, from a single in-house team.

About Us FAQs

Is Expo Stalls Fabrication a registered company?

Yes. Expo Stalls Fabrication has served clients since 2012 and was formally registered with the Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan (SECP) in 2017.

Where are Expo Stalls Fabrication's offices located?

The head office is in Karachi, with regional offices in Lahore and Islamabad. Fabrication takes place at the Karachi workshop, with installation carried out nationwide.

Do you design, fabricate and install everything in-house?

Yes. Design, carpentry, metalwork, printing, finishing, transport and installation are all handled by our own team rather than outsourced to separate contractors.

What industries has Expo Stalls Fabrication worked with?

Documented projects include fragrance and beauty brands, manufacturing and industrial companies, and travel and tourism organisations, among others.

How can I see examples of past work?

Our portfolio and case studies pages show fabricated stalls and, where available, before-and-after photos from the workshop to the show floor.
